My Top 5 Movies

  1. Interstellar (2014): An unforgettable blend of ecological urgency, relativity, and Hans Zimmer's score that makes space exploration feel deeply human.
  2. Shutter Island (2010): A masterclass in psychological tension and unreliable narration that rewards watching a second time to catch every clue.
  3. The Godfather (1972): Unmatched character development, pacing, and storytelling about power, loyalty, and consequence.
  4. How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days (2003): Effortless comedic timing and sharp chemistry that always holds up when you want something light and entertaining.
  5. Kingsman: The Secret Service (2014): Stylishly choreographed action paired with sharp British humour and a fresh spin on classic spy tropes.
